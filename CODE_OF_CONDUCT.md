# Code of Conduct

We want Shortlist to be a welcoming project for everyone, including people who are job hunting
and under pressure.

**Expected:** be kind and patient, assume good intent, give constructive feedback, respect
different experience levels and backgrounds, and protect other people's privacy.

**Not accepted:** harassment, insults, discriminatory language, personal attacks, sharing
someone's personal data without consent, or sustained disruption.

**Enforcement:** maintainers may edit or remove contributions and warn, suspend or ban
participants who break this code. Report problems privately to the maintainers through the
contact listed on the repository profile. Reports are handled confidentially.

This code is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
version 2.1.
